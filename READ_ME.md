Project Title: Can Climate Shape Bee Sociality Through Floral Resource Availability?

Description: This project wanted to answer the question is whether the ENSO phenomenon or other environmental factors account for the degree of sociability in the bee species under study.

The Final_Project repository contains four main folders: 
- Data
- Documents
- Outputs
- R_scripts
In my Data folder, I have two additional folders: one for the raw data and the other for the processed data.
In my Documents folder, I have two folders: one for a folder for my manuscript
In my Outputs folder, I have three folders: one for the exploratory statistics, the other for the graphs, and the last one for the output of the models.
In my third folder, I have two folders: one for my original script in R, and the other for the new ones.

I obtain the ENSO data from the NOAA web Page here: https://www.psl.noaa.gov/enso/mei/, and the environmental measurements of the BCI research station from here: https://smithsonian.figshare.com/articles/dataset/Monthly_Summary_BCI_horizontal/10059455/6?file=22904153
